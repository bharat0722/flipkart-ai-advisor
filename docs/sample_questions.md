# Sample questions

One question per line, prefixed with a category tag. `scripts/run_questions.py` reads this file.

## Budget
- Best headphones under 1000 rupees
- Show me a laptop between 20000 and 40000
- Cheapest speakers you have

## Category
- Laptop for students
- Do you have any cameras?
- Good wireless earphones with a mic

## Comparison and advice
- Compare the top two Bluetooth speakers
- Which pen drive is best for storing movies?
- Is there a gaming laptop in the catalogue?

## Multi-part
- Which laptop is cheaper, and how much RAM and storage does each one have?
- Find a pen drive under 600 rupees and tell me its capacity and price
- Show me a wireless headphone under 1000 and a Bluetooth speaker under 1000, and say which costs less
- What is the cheapest power bank, and is there a router under 1500?
- Recommend a laptop for students and a mouse to go with it
- Do you have a gaming laptop, and if not, which laptop has the best graphics?

## Follow-up (uses the previous question)
- Show me wireless headphones under 2000
- Which is the best?
- What about under 1000?

## Out of scope / edge cases
- What is the capital of France?
- Do you sell refrigerators?
- Laptop under 100 rupees

## Thin categories (answers may use labelled synthetic records)
- Which laptop is best for gaming?
- Cheapest laptop you have
- Compare the Veltrix Student Lite and the Veltrix Scholar laptops
- Recommend a wireless mouse under 1000 rupees
- Best keyboard for gaming
- Which pen drive has the most storage for movies?
