# Sample questions

One question per line, prefixed with a category tag. `scripts/run_questions.py` reads this file.

## Budget
- Best headphones under 1000 rupees
- Show me a laptop between 20000 and 40000
- Cheapest speakers you have

## Category
- Laptop for students
- Do you have any cameras?
- Good wireless earphones with a mic

## Comparison and advice
- Compare the top two Bluetooth speakers
- Which pen drive is best for storing movies?
- Is there a gaming laptop in the catalogue?

## Follow-up (uses the previous question)
- Show me wireless headphones under 2000
- Which is the best?
- What about under 1000?

## Out of scope / edge cases
- What is the capital of France?
- Do you sell refrigerators?
- Laptop under 100 rupees
